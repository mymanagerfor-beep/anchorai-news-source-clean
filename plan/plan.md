# AnchorAI News — Gujarati AI News-Anchor Video Generator

A multi-tenant SaaS that turns an authorized anchor portrait and a Gujarati news script into a branded 16:9 "Breaking News" MP4. The anchor speaks the script with lip-sync and has Gujarati subtitles, a ticker and a visible AI-disclosure label.
Every video is really generated by an AI provider and really rendered. Nothing in the app is mocked, faked or pre-baked.

## Who it's for
- Gujarati digital news channels that need fast presenter-led bulletins.
- Independent news creators and YouTubers who don't have a studio or an on-camera anchor.
- Regional newsroom teams producing several short updates a day.
- Agencies that run several news brands from one workspace.

## Core features and experience
- **Accounts and workspaces.** Users sign up with email and password, verify their email, and can reset a forgotten password. A workspace is created at sign-up and the user owns it. Sessions expire, and signing out revokes the session. Each workspace's data is fully isolated from every other workspace.
- **Dashboard.** All numbers come from real records: videos generated, processing, completed and failed, plus credit balance, recent projects and recent credit transactions.
- **Anchor library.** Users upload a JPG, PNG or WebP portrait (up to 10 MB, at least 512 px on the short side). The file is checked by its contents, not just its extension. Users add a display name, a description and who owns or authorized the likeness. They then confirm a versioned consent statement and choose a basis for it:
  - "This is me"
  - "Employee or talent with a signed release"
  - "Contracted or licensed presenter"

  The consent record stores the user, workspace, asset, consent-text version, time and basis. A portrait without valid consent can never be animated. Users can revoke consent, which blocks future use and fails any queued job safely, and they can delete the asset. The app says plainly that revocation can't recall videos already downloaded.
- **Channels (brand presets).** A workspace can save several channels. Each channel has a logo (transparent PNG or WebP), a name, primary and secondary colors, a logo corner, a subtitle style and a default ticker. Agencies pick a channel for each project.
- **News video creator (7-step wizard):**
  1. **Select anchor.** Only consented portraits are shown.
  2. **Script.** A large Gujarati editor with:
     - character count, word count and estimated narration time
     - script validation (maximum 60 seconds of narration per video)
     - optional headline and location
     - pronunciation notes, as "word → how to say it" pairs. These change only the spoken narration. Subtitles keep the original spelling.
     - save as draft
  3. **Narration.** Language is fixed to Gujarati (gu-IN). The user picks from a small, curated list of ElevenLabs voices that were checked with a real Gujarati sample. "Preview voice" plays a real, short Gujarati clip. Speech speed is not offered because the provider doesn't support it, and the app says so.
  4. **Template.** Breaking News is selected and is the only template for now.
  5. **Video settings.** Fixed at 16:9, 1920×1080, MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio). Subtitles and branding are on by default. The app notes that the anchor is generated at the provider's resolution and then composed onto a 1080p canvas.
  6. **Branding.** Logo, channel name, colors, BREAKING NEWS banner text, headline, location label, scrolling ticker, subtitle style (size, color, background or outline, position), optional intro and outro title cards (about 3 seconds each, showing logo and channel name) and an optional Gujarati date label. A live static preview shows the real portrait with the entered text. It is clearly labelled "Static preview, not the generated video."
  7. **Consent and generate.** The user re-confirms permission to animate this person and use the generated likeness and voice. The screen shows the estimated credit charge, the expected processing time and the synthetic-media notice. A job is submitted only after every check passes: consent, verified email, script length, credits and provider configuration.
- **Real generation pipeline:**
  1. Gujarati narration is generated with ElevenLabs v3, which also returns word timestamps.
  2. The portrait and the narration go to Kling AI Avatar v2 for a lip-synced talking-anchor video.
  3. The app retrieves the video and checks it: duration, dimensions, audio stream, and that it decodes.
  4. The app renders the final 1920×1080 broadcast frame with:
     - the anchor on a branded studio backdrop
     - the logo
     - the red BREAKING NEWS banner
     - the Gujarati headline lower-third and location
     - burned-in Gujarati subtitles timed from the narration timestamps
     - the scrolling ticker
     - intro and outro cards
     - the label "AI દ્વારા બનાવાયેલ પ્રસ્તુતકર્તા વિડિયો / AI-generated presenter video"

     Text never covers the anchor's face, and safe margins are respected.
  5. The final file is checked, then stored privately and verified as readable.
  6. Only then is the job marked completed. The user gets an email saying the video is ready.
- **Jobs page.** Shows job ID, project, submission time, anchor, template, internal status and the provider's own status. Internal statuses are: draft, queued, validating, generating, retrieving, rendering, completed, failed, cancelled. It also shows estimated and final credits, and a clear error message when something fails. Users can retry when it's safe, which reuses any narration or anchor video already produced so the provider isn't paid twice. Users can cancel at any stage. Completed videos can be previewed and downloaded.
- **Video library.** Users can preview and download videos, see the script and settings used, see the credits charged, rename projects, delete videos and inspect failures. Subtitles can be exported as SRT and WebVTT. Downloads go through short-lived, authorized links, and no file is ever publicly exposed.
- **Credits and billing:**
  - Every credit change is recorded in an append-only ledger. Entry types are purchase, trial, reservation, capture, release, refund and admin adjustment.
  - At submit, the estimate plus a 15% buffer is reserved atomically, so two jobs can't spend the same credits.
  - On completion, only the actual seconds are captured (rounded up, minimum 10) and the rest is released.
  - Credits are released in full on any failure or cancellation.
  - Retries and duplicate events never charge twice.
  - Credit packs are bought through Stripe Checkout. Credits are added only after the server confirms the payment, either through a signed webhook or a direct check with Stripe.
  - The billing page shows the balance, packs, usage history, payment records and Stripe receipts.
- **Account and privacy.** Users can delete their account, request data deletion, and see the consent and deletion history for their assets.
- **Admin.** A seeded admin account can check whether the AI provider is configured and reachable, adjust credits (a reason is required and the change is audited) and view audit events. If the provider isn't configured, an explicit setup error is shown. The app never pretends a job succeeded.
- **Landing page.** Covers the product introduction, the workflow in steps, features, credit-pack pricing, supported language and output (Gujarati, 16:9 1080p MP4), sign-up and sign-in, and a clear notice that presenters and videos are AI-generated. There are no sample videos.

## User flow
1. A visitor reads the landing page and signs up. A workspace is created and 30 trial credits are added. The visitor verifies their email.
2. In the Anchor library, they upload a portrait, fill in the authorization details and confirm consent.
3. In Channels, they save their logo, colors and default ticker.
4. They open New Video and work through the steps: pick the anchor, write the Gujarati script, pick and preview a voice, keep Breaking News at 16:9, adjust branding against the live static preview, then confirm consent and see the credit estimate.
5. They click Generate. The job appears on the Jobs page and moves through the statuses in real time.
6. When rendering finishes, they get an email and an in-app notice. They preview the MP4, download it and export subtitles.
7. The billing page shows the reservation, capture and release entries. If a job failed, it shows the full release and the reason.
8. When credits run low, they buy a pack through Stripe and the balance updates after the payment is verified.

## UI/UX feel
- A premium newsroom production console: dark navy and charcoal surfaces, white type and sharp red breaking-news accents.
- Restrained gradients, clean cards and generous spacing. No decorative widgets and no busy animation.
- Gujarati text uses a proper Gujarati typeface so conjunct letters render correctly in the editor, the previews and the final video.
- Desktop-first wizard with a step rail and a sticky composition preview. It adapts to tablet and mobile.
- Clear status chips and progress for every job state. Error messages tell the user what to do next.

## Assumptions
- **Clarifying questions skipped.** The user skipped them, so the recommended defaults were applied.
- **AI providers.** fal.ai runs through the Emergent Universal Key, so no external account or key is needed. Narration uses ElevenLabs v3 with the language forced to Gujarati. The talking anchor uses Kling AI Avatar v2 Standard, which is listed for commercial use.
  - Provider cost is roughly US$0.06–0.07 per second of video. It is drawn from the owner's Universal Key balance.
  - If the balance runs out, jobs fail clearly and credits are released.
  - Gujarati output is checked with a live sample during the build. If Gujarati narration is ever unavailable, generation is blocked before any credits are reserved. The app never falls back to Hindi or English.
- **Provider updates are polled.** Job status is checked with the provider on a short interval. Webhooks aren't delivered to the app in this mode. The avatar webhook endpoint is kept for future direct-key providers.
- **Sending files to the provider.** The portrait and narration are sent to the provider inline or through provider-hosted links. App storage is never made public.
- **Script limit.** Up to 60 seconds of narration per video (configurable). Gujarati narration speed for the estimate is configurable.
- **Pricing.** 1 credit = 1 second of finished narration (minimum 10 per video). Packs are priced in INR:
  - Starter: 120 credits for ₹1,199
  - Newsroom: 600 credits for ₹4,999
  - Agency: 1,800 credits for ₹13,499

  Pack prices and per-second rates are set on the server. Prices are one-time; there are no subscriptions in Phase 1.
- **Trial credits.** New workspaces get 30 trial credits, enough for one 30-second bulletin, usable only after email verification. This limits abuse of the provider cost.
- **Refund policy.** All reserved credits are released on any failure or cancellation, including failures after the provider was paid; the platform absorbs that cost. Stripe card refunds are handled manually by the admin.
- **Payments and tax.** Stripe runs as a claimable test sandbox, and the owner claims it to take live payments. By default Stripe manages tax and compliance where eligible (+3.5% per transaction). The alternatives are "Stripe calculates tax only" (+0.5%) and "no tax help".
- **Email.** Verification, password-reset and "video ready" emails are sent through Emergent's managed email service under the "AnchorAI News" name.
- **Allowed consent bases.** Only the three listed. Public figures and politicians may not be animated unless the uploader is that person. Impersonation for deception is prohibited in the terms shown at consent.
- **Anchor framing.** Best results come from a front-facing, shoulders-up portrait. The anchor is framed on a branded studio backdrop, with all graphics kept below and beside the face.
- **Interface language.** The interface is in English. All content (script, headline, ticker, subtitles, disclosure) is Gujarati-first.
- **Workspace membership.** One owner per workspace in Phase 1. Invitations come in Phase 2.
- **Stack changes from the brief, made for the hosting platform:**
  - The platform's managed document database replaces PostgreSQL. Its indexes and unique constraints enforce isolation and idempotency.
  - A built-in, crash-resumable background worker replaces Redis.
  - Emergent private object storage replaces S3. It has no presigned URLs, so downloads go through short-lived authorized links served by the app.
  - The platform's React setup replaces Vite/TypeScript.
  - Docker files are provided for self-hosting but are not used on this platform.
